ABOUT FC MAPS
{% block about_intro %}

FC Maps is an interactive atlas of professional football and a product of TdB GEO. The current edition focuses on the FIFA World Cup 2026, plotting where every player in every qualified national squad was born.

{% endblock %}

The related database combines squad lists with geocoded birthplaces, club affiliations, and current season rosters. Each marker on the map represents one player. Click on a marker to see the player's details.

Data sources. Squad and biographical information was collected from publicly available sources such as Wikipedia, Transfermarkt and other data sources. Birthplace coordinates were derived using the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoder, supplemented with manual corrections where the geocoder could not resolve a location. Country borders come from Natural Earth.

Methodology. Players born in countries that no longer exist (Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia, Zaire) retain their historical country name as recorded at the time of birth, while the geographic coordinates fall within the modern successor state. Coastal and island birthplaces are matched within a 5 km tolerance of national borders.
